What we do

Services built around the full life of a site.

From first survey to final rehabilitation, Excavate manages every stage of a mining operation under one roof — so partners deal with a single, accountable team.

01

Extraction

Surface and underground extraction using equipment matched to each site's geology, ore body, and yield profile. Drilling, blasting, and haulage planned around minimising waste rock.

02

Processing

On-site crushing, screening, and beneficiation to bring raw material to market-ready grade before it ever leaves the site.

03

Logistics

Rail and road haulage contracts, stockpile management, and port scheduling for partners who need predictable delivery windows.

04

Site survey & assessment

Geological survey, resource estimation, and environmental impact assessment before any ground is broken.

05

Safety consulting

Independent safety audits and training programmes, drawing on Excavate's own site protocols for outside operators.

06

Rehabilitation

Land restoration and closure planning, built into every contract from the first day of operation rather than added at the end.

How we work

One contract, one point of contact.

Most partners engage Excavate for a single stage — processing, or logistics — but the same project manager stays assigned from survey through closure, so nothing gets lost between handoffs.
